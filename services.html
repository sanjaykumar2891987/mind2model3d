Technologies

Printing Methods

FDM

Fused Deposition Modeling

The most versatile method. Great for functional parts, prototypes, and large objects. Available in PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU, and more.

  • ✓ Large build volume
  • ✓ Wide material range
  • ✓ Cost-effective
  • ✓ Fast turnaround
RESIN

Resin (MSLA/DLP)

Ultra-high detail printing for miniatures, jewelry, and anything requiring fine features. Smooth surface finish straight off the printer.

  • ✓ Ultra-fine detail
  • ✓ Smooth surfaces
  • ✓ Perfect for miniatures
  • ✓ Jewelry-grade precision
SLA

Stereolithography

Professional-grade prints for engineering applications, dental models, and high-accuracy prototypes requiring the best surface quality.

  • ✓ Highest accuracy
  • ✓ Engineering-grade
  • ✓ Premium finish
  • ✓ Wide resin library
